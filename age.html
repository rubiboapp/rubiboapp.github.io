Age Calculator

Calculate your exact age in years, months and days from your date of birth.

How does the age calculator work?

Enter your date of birth and the calculator compares it with today's date to calculate your current age in years, months and days.

What can you use it for?

You can use an age calculator to check your exact age, calculate someone's age for a birthday or quickly work out how old a person is on a particular date.

Accurate age calculation

The calculation takes the different lengths of calendar months into account instead of simply dividing the number of days by 365.
