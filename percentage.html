Percentage Calculator

Calculate percentages quickly and easily. Find the percentage of any number with this free online percentage calculator.

How to calculate a percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide the result by 100.

Example

If you want to calculate 20% of 150, enter 20 as the percentage and 150 as the number. The result is 30.

What can you use this calculator for?

This percentage calculator can be used for everyday calculations, school work, business calculations, discounts, price changes and many other situations where you need to calculate a percentage quickly.
