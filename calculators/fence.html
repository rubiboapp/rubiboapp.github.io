Fence Calculator

Estimate how many fence posts and sections you need for your fencing project.

How many fence posts do I need?

Enter the total length of your fence and the desired spacing between posts. The calculator estimates the number of fence sections and posts required.

Fence post spacing

The correct spacing depends on the type of fence, the materials used and local building requirements. Eight feet is commonly used as a starting point for many fencing projects, but you should check the requirements for your specific fence.

What else should I consider?

When planning a fence, remember to account for gates, corners, changes in direction and the actual dimensions of your fence panels or sections.

This calculator provides an estimate and should be used as a planning tool rather than a substitute for local building requirements or manufacturer instructions.
