Board Foot Calculator

Calculate board feet for lumber using thickness, width and length. You can also estimate the total cost of your wood.

What is a board foot?

A board foot is a standard unit used to measure the volume of lumber. One board foot is equal to a board that is 1 inch thick, 12 inches wide and 1 foot long.

How to calculate board feet

To calculate board feet, multiply the thickness in inches by the width in inches and the length in feet, then divide the result by 12.

Why use a board foot calculator?

A board foot calculator can help woodworkers, carpenters and lumber buyers estimate how much wood they need and how much their lumber will cost.
