Gravel Calculator

Calculate how much gravel you need for your driveway, patio, walkway or landscaping project.

How much gravel do I need?

To estimate the amount of gravel required, calculate the area of your project and multiply it by the desired gravel depth.

This gravel calculator adds an optional waste allowance so you can account for compaction, uneven ground and material loss during installation.

Gravel calculator formula

The basic calculation is length × width × depth. The result gives you the approximate volume of gravel required.

Common gravel projects

You can use this calculator for driveways, walkways, patios, garden paths, landscaping areas and other projects that require a layer of gravel or aggregate.
