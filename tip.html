Tip Calculator

Calculate the tip, total bill and amount each person should pay when splitting a restaurant bill.

How to calculate a tip

Enter the bill amount, choose the tip percentage and enter the number of people sharing the bill. The calculator will show the tip, total bill and amount per person.

Example

For an $80 bill with a 15% tip, the tip is $12 and the total is $92. If two people split the bill, each person pays $46.

Why use a tip calculator?

A tip calculator makes it easy to work out restaurant tips and split bills without doing the calculation manually.
